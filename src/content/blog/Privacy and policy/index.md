## 1. Introduction

BusKhoja ("the Service," "the Application," "we," "us," or "our") is a transportation and navigation platform developed by Ruhan Bin Rouf under the organization TheKami. BusKhoja originated as a student-led initiative under the **Asset Programme** at **MAWTS Institute of Technology, Pallabi, Dhaka, Bangladesh**, and has since grown into a publicly available service designed to simplify urban commuting across Dhaka. It provides users with comprehensive bus route information, real-time fare data, and intuitive navigation tools.

BusKhoja is available across two platforms:
- **Web Application:** [https://www.buskhoja.com](https://www.buskhoja.com)
- **Android Application:** Available on the Google Play Store

We also maintain a blog at [https://blog.buskhoja.com](https://blog.buskhoja.com) for service updates, transport insights, and announcements.

This Privacy Policy describes how BusKhoja collects, uses, stores, and safeguards information when you use either the web or Android version of our Service. It also outlines your rights with respect to your data and explains how we handle information in compliance with applicable data protection regulations.

By accessing the BusKhoja website, blog, or Android application, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the Service immediately.

This Privacy Policy applies to all BusKhoja platforms (web and Android). It does not govern third-party websites, services, or applications that may be linked to or integrated with BusKhoja, each of which operates under its own privacy terms.

## 2. Information We Collect

BusKhoja is designed with privacy in mind. The Application does not require account creation, user registration, or submission of personal profile information. We collect only the minimum information necessary to deliver a functional, reliable, and accurate transportation service.

The following categories of information may be collected when you use BusKhoja:

### 2.1 Location Data

- **Precise geographic location** of your device, collected when you grant location permission, in order to provide nearby bus route information, stop identification, real-time bus position display, and location-based navigation assistance.
- Location access applies to **two categories of users:** passengers using BusKhoja for commute navigation, and bus operators (drivers) whose position and speed data is surfaced to passengers through the Application.
- Location data continues to be processed via a background foreground service even when the Application is not in the foreground, for the purpose of delivering uninterrupted real-time transit information. A persistent system notification is displayed at all times during background tracking, which the user may dismiss to immediately terminate location access.
- BusKhoja does not observe, store, or have visibility into any individual user's live location. All location processing for real-time features is handled natively on-device or anonymously at the infrastructure level.

### 2.2 Device and Technical Information

- Android operating system version
- Device manufacturer and model
- Application version number
- Crash logs and diagnostic data
- General performance metrics required for service reliability and issue resolution

### 2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

BusKhoja does **not** collect, store, or process any of the following:

- Usernames or passwords
- Email addresses or phone numbers
- Personal profile or biographical information
- Payment or financial data
- Photographs, media files, or contacts
- Browsing history or activity outside of the Application

## 4. Location Data

### 4.1 Purpose of Location Access

BusKhoja requests access to your device's location to deliver its core real-time transportation features. Location data is used to:

- Identify bus stops and routes in proximity to your current position
- Display real-time bus positions on the map for passengers
- Surface bus operator speed and live position to commuters
- Provide accurate navigation guidance for public transport
- Improve the relevance of route and stop suggestions based on your area
- Support emergency feature services, including identifying nearby hospitals and fire stations relative to your location

### 4.2 Who Is Subject to Location Tracking

Location tracking within BusKhoja applies to two distinct categories of users:

- **Passengers:** Location is used to identify nearby buses, relevant stops, and to assist with navigation while commuting.
- **Bus Operators (Drivers):** Location and speed data from bus operators is processed by the Application and surfaced anonymously to passengers to enable real-time bus tracking. Bus operators are expected to be made aware of this data use by the entity deploying BusKhoja for operational purposes.

### 4.3 Background Location — Foreground Service

When you grant location permission and begin using BusKhoja's real-time transit features, the Application may continue processing your location data even after you exit the active screen or minimize the Application. This is handled via an **Android foreground service**, which operates as follows:

- A **persistent system notification** is displayed on your device at all times while background location access is active, clearly indicating that BusKhoja is running in the background.
- You may **tap and dismiss this notification** at any time to immediately stop background location access without opening the Application.
- Background location is used solely to maintain continuity of real-time transit features and is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to transportation services.

This background access requires and operates under the `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION` permission on Android, which must be explicitly granted by the user.

### 4.4 BusKhoja Does Not Monitor Individual Locations

BusKhoja is designed such that **no member of our team, organization, or infrastructure has visibility into the live or historical location of any individual user.** Location data is processed natively at the device level or handled anonymously at the infrastructure level to power Application features. We do not build individual location profiles, track user movement patterns, or store personally identifiable location histories.

### 4.5 Permission and Control

Location access is fully permission-based. You may:

- Grant or deny location permission when first prompted by the Application
- Revoke location permission at any time via your Android device's **Settings → Apps → BusKhoja → Permissions**
- Stop background location access at any time by **dismissing the foreground service notification**

Denying or revoking location permission will disable real-time transit features but will not affect the use of BusKhoja's static route and information functions.

### 4.6 Retention of Location Data

BusKhoja does not retain precise individual location data beyond the immediate real-time requirements of the active session. No historical location trail linked to your device or identity is stored on our servers.

## 6. Notifications

BusKhoja uses two distinct types of notifications:

### 6.1 Foreground Service Notification

When background location access is active, the Application displays a **persistent foreground service notification** on your Android device. This notification:

- Is required by Android to indicate that a background service is running
- Clearly identifies BusKhoja as the source of background location access
- Can be **dismissed by the user at any time** to immediately stop background location tracking
- Is not a marketing or promotional message

### 6.2 General Push Notifications

BusKhoja may send informational push notifications for the following purposes:

- **Service updates:** Changes to bus routes, schedules, or fare information relevant to your usage
- **Maintenance notices:** Planned or unplanned service interruptions affecting Application functionality
- **Feature announcements:** Information about new features, improvements, or updates to the Application
- **Emergency service information:** Updates related to the Application's emergency features, including additions of new emergency contacts or facility data
- **General transport information:** Relevant announcements related to the transportation services covered by BusKhoja

### 6.3 Managing Notifications

You retain full control over push notifications at all times. Notifications may be disabled or customized through your Android device's notification settings. Disabling general push notifications will not impair the core functionality of BusKhoja. The foreground service notification cannot be permanently suppressed while background location access is active, as it is required by the Android operating system.

## 7. Emergency Features

BusKhoja includes a dedicated emergency assistance module designed to provide users with rapid access to critical services. These features are currently available on the BusKhoja web platform at [https://www.buskhoja.com](https://www.buskhoja.com) and are confirmed for inclusion in a forthcoming update to the Android Application.

### 7.1 Features Included

- **Emergency Call (999):** A direct shortcut to initiate an emergency call to Bangladesh's national emergency number (999) from within the Application.
- **Nearby Hospitals:** Displays hospitals in proximity to the user's current location to assist in medical emergencies.
- **Nearby Fire Stations:** Displays fire stations near the user's location along with their contact numbers.

### 7.2 Location Use for Emergency Features

Emergency feature results (e.g., nearby hospitals and fire stations) are generated using your device's current location at the time of the request. This location access:

- Is used solely to calculate proximity to relevant emergency services
- Is not stored, logged, or transmitted beyond the scope of generating the immediate response
- Operates under the same location permission already granted to the Application

### 7.3 Nature of Emergency Information

Emergency service data (hospital names, fire station numbers, addresses) displayed within BusKhoja is sourced from publicly available information and mapping data. BusKhoja does not guarantee the real-time accuracy or availability of emergency services listed. In a genuine emergency, always contact 999 or the relevant authority directly. BusKhoja's emergency feature is an informational aid and does not replace official emergency services.
