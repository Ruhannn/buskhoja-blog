## Overview

Buskhoja.xyz is a cutting-edge platform designed to streamline bus travel in Dhaka, Bangladesh. Serving a huge daily commuters, it delivers accurate, up-to-date information on fares, routes, and schedules sourced from official authorities and community contributions. Our slogan: "In times of danger, every second counts, so we strive to be fast and perfect from all angles."

## Key Features

- **Real-Time Fare Information**: Access fares ranging from ৳10 to ৳350, available 24/7 for informed decision-making.
- **Advanced Route Planning**: Plan journeys between major hubs, such as Mirpur to Airport (starting at ৳20), with detailed stop-by-stop guidance.
- **Smart Search Functionality**: Easily search by bus name, route number, keywords, or destinations for quick results.
- **Extensive Coverage**: Over 200 bus routes mapped across Dhaka, ensuring comprehensive support for all commuters.
- **Community-Driven Updates**: Leverages user input and official data for ongoing accuracy and reliability.

## How It Works

Users simply input their starting point and destination via the intuitive search interface. The platform instantly provides optimal routes, estimated fares, and alternative options, making navigation effortless in Dhaka's dynamic transport landscape.

## Benefits for Commuters

- **Time Efficiency**: Reduce waiting times with precise route details and real-time updates.
- **Cost Savings**: Compare fares across routes to choose the most economical option.
- **Safety and Convenience**: Integrated emergency services, like ambulance and fire routes, enhance user security.
- **Accessibility**: Mobile-friendly design ensures easy access on the go.
